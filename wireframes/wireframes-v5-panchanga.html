Section 3 — Yoga quality tiers

27 Yogas grouped into 4 quality tiers. The tier determines the left bar color, name color, and background tint in both the collapsed card and the expanded table.

Yoga quality tiers (27 total)

Quality tiers control bar color, name color, and row background tint. Only the active yoga fires the warning state; past/future yogas are always shown at 38% opacity.

Tier Color Yogas Card behavior
Excellent #C8922A amber Brahma, Indra, Siddha, Siddhi, Amrit (if applicable) Amber bar + name; faint amber bg tint
Auspicious #2A8A5A green Priti, Ayushman, Saubhagya, Shobhana, Sukarman, Dhriti, Vriddhi, Dhruva, Harshana, Vriddhi, Variyana, Shiva, Sadhya, Shubha, Shukla Green bar + name; faint green bg tint
Neutral #8890A8 gray Atiganda, Shula, Ganda, Vyaghata, Vajra Gray bar; name in default ink color; no tint
Inauspicious #B84040 red Vishkambha, Vyatipata, Parigha, Vaidhriti Red bar + name + red bg tint; card border turns red

Karana types (11 types, ~60 per lunar month)

Karanas cycle through types twice per tithi (half-tithi each). Of the 11 types, only Vishti (Bhadra) is inauspicious. The other 10 are auspicious or neutral.

Tier Karanas Notes
Auspicious Bava, Balava, Kaulava, Taitila, Garaja, Vanija 6 moveable karanas (most common)
Inauspicious Vishti (Bhadra) 7th moveable karana. Bhadra Kaal — card turns red, same treatment as Rahu Kaal
Neutral Shakuni, Chatushpada, Naga, Kimstughna 4 fixed karanas; occur only on Amavasya period

Section 5 — Open design questions

Items requiring a decision before implementation begins.

Q1 — Vara sub-label: ruling planet name or full vara name?

In the expanded Vara row, the sub-label shows "Guruvara · ruled by Jupiter" (English) or "गुरुवार" (Hindi).
Option A: Show the Sanskrit vara name as the sub-label ("Guruvara", "Shukravara", etc.) — culturally precise, familiar to Jyotish practitioners.
Option B: Just show the ruling planet ("ruled by Jupiter") — more accessible to casual users.

Q2 — Tithi and Nakshatra in expanded view: redundant or necessary?

The expanded panchanga table repeats Tithi and Nakshatra data already shown prominently above the cards.
Option A: Include all 5 for traditional completeness — users expect panchanga to have exactly 5 elements.
Option B: Show only Yoga + Karana (the new elements) in the expanded view; label the card "Yoga & Karana" not "Full Panchanga". Simpler, no visual redundancy.

Q3 — Collapse persistence

Should the expanded/collapsed state be persisted across app restarts (via SharedPreferences), or always reset to collapsed on launch?
Recommendation: always reset to collapsed — keeps the day view compact by default, matching the Muhurta screen's night choghadiya pattern.
