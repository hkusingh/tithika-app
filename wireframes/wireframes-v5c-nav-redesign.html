Design decisions captured

Why dropdown, not drawer? A full-screen drawer feels heavy for four items. The dropdown appears inline below the nav and keeps the Day View context visible behind it (dimmed). It dismisses with a tap anywhere outside — familiar iOS/Android pattern.

Why keep Muhurta card off Day View? Day View's job is "what is today" — Tithi, Nakshatra, sunrise/sunset, current Hora. Choghadiya requires explaining 8 time slots; that belongs on the Muhurta screen. The Rahu row on the Hora card gives the one actionable warning without importing the full Choghadiya UI into Day View.

Why "ends HH:MM" not countdown for active Rahu? A clock time is stable — the user can read it, put the phone down, and refer back. A countdown (34 min left) requires a live rebuild every minute and is confusing if the screen isn't refreshed. Clock time wins for a warning that lasts 90 minutes.

Hamburger icon choice. ☰ (trigram / three horizontal lines) is universally understood as "more menu". Alternatives like ⊕ or ● were considered but ☰ has the strongest affordance for navigation expansion on mobile.
