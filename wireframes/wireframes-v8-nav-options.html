Summary — Three directions at a glance

Option A (Bottom Tab Bar) — Most conventional mobile pattern. Festivals and Muhurta are always visible and one tap from anywhere. Removes the day strip entirely; date navigation becomes swipe-only. Best discoverability; smallest bottom chrome (58px). Panchanga needs a secondary home (inside Muhurta or Calendar).

Option B (Dock + Slim Strip) — Keeps tap-to-jump date navigation with a simpler strip (dates only). Adds a compact dock above it. All 4 secondary screens — including Panchanga — are exposed at a glance. Slightly taller combined chrome (~88px). Best coverage of all screens.

Option C (Feature Cards in Home) — No navigation changes. Festivals and Muhurta appear as live data cards when you scroll past Hora on the home screen. Least chrome (44px strip). Most contextual — shows real data not just labels. Least discoverable — nothing tells you to scroll down.

All three: hamburger removed · top nav = 🏠 · TITHIKA · 🗓 · ⚙ · strip simplified to dates only.
