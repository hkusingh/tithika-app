Tithi calculations depend on your location. We use it to determine local sunrise, which sets each day's tithi.
Notes

  • "Enter manually" → searchable city picker (next screen, not shown)
  • "Skip" sets location to Ujjain (classical reference meridian) with a persistent banner on main screen
