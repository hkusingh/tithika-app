Executive Summary

The Hindu calendar app market is served by several well-established players, most of which have been built over many years and accumulated large user bases — particularly Drik Panchang, which is the category leader. The common pattern across all competitors is a heavy feature load: full Panchanga, Kundali, horoscopes, muhurta calculators, regional calendars, and often prayers and aarti content. This breadth comes at a cost — cluttered interfaces, aggressive advertising, and a steep learning curve for new users.

Tithika's opportunity is to occupy a clearly differentiated position: the most beautiful, most accurate, and most approachable tithi-first app. No ads. No astrology. No feature bloat. Tithika bets that a large segment of users — especially the diaspora and younger urban Indians — wants a calm, well-designed, trustworthy lunar calendar without wading through Kundali charts or Vastu compasses.

Competitor Profiles

1. Drik Panchang — Hindu Calendar 4.8 ★ iOS (5.5K reviews) · 4.6 ★ Android (198K reviews) · 1M+ downloads
Platform: iOS & Android Price: Free + ads; ad-free ₹19/mo or ₹199/yr Developer: Adarsh Mobile Applications LLP Last updated: February 2026

Drik Panchang is the de-facto reference standard in the category, backed by a reputed website (drikpanchang.com) with decades of trust. Its Android app alone has over a million downloads and nearly 200,000 ratings. The feature set is encyclopedic: from basic Panchanga through Kundali generation, horoscope matching, and a Vastu compass. For a devout user who wants everything in one place, it is the default choice. However, that same completeness is its biggest usability liability. Reviews consistently praise accuracy while criticising the ad experience and interface complexity. It has no visual representation of the moon and its onboarding assumes prior knowledge of Vedic concepts.

Strengths

  • Undisputed accuracy benchmark — Drik calculation method, trusted by astrologers
  • Full Panchanga: all 5 elements plus extended Dainika data
  • Kundali generator (D1–D60 charts), horoscope matching, Vimshottari Dasha
  • 10+ Indian languages: Bengali, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Tamil, Telugu
  • Offline-first; location-aware globally; DST support built in
  • Cloud sync; festival reminders; ICS calendar export
  • Vastu compass, Prashnavali, Choghadiya, Hora, Rahu Kalam with countdown timers
  • Active development — updated February 2026

Weaknesses / Gaps

  • UI is dense and dated — information overload for casual or new users
  • Aggressive ads on free tier (full-screen interstitials between tabs)
  • No moon-phase visualisation — tithi is a number, not a visual experience
  • No home-screen widget on iOS
  • Privacy: collects identifiers and usage data for cross-app tracking
  • iOS app's last major feature update was 2019
  • Onboarding assumes familiarity with Panchanga concepts
2. Shubh Panchang — Hindu Calendar ~4.5 ★ · 1 lakh+ daily users · iOS & Android
Platform: iOS & Android + web (shubhpanchang.com) Price: Free (ad-supported) Developer: AppStoneLab Technologies LLP

Shubh Panchang positions itself as the friendlier, more modern alternative to Drik Panchang. Its clean interface and emphasis on muhurta timings resonate with users planning important life events. The app operates as both a mobile app and web property. The muhurta module is regarded as one of the best in the category. Its weakness is depth of festival coverage and the lack of brand trust that Drik Panchang has built over many years. Some users report festival dates missing or incorrect — a serious issue for a devotional app.

Strengths

  • Cleaner, more modern UI — praised as beginner-friendly
  • Strong muhurta: Gruh Pravesh, marriage, vehicle, property timings
  • Full Panchanga plus Choghadiya, Hora, Gowri Panchang
  • Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda, Abhijit Muhurta with location-aware timings
  • Both Purnimanta and Amanta month systems
  • Festival & vrat notifications; personalized horoscopes
  • Web version extends reach beyond mobile

Weaknesses / Gaps

  • Festival coverage gaps — Dussehra missing in some versions
  • Ad-supported free model; not ad-free by default
  • No moon-phase visualisation
  • No home-screen widgets
  • Location defaulted to Mumbai in some versions
  • English/Hindi/Gujarati only — narrower than Drik Panchang
  • Less established accuracy trust than Drik
3. Hindu Calendar — Panchang ~4.5 ★ · #1 in 50+ countries on iOS · 2 lakh+ users
Platform: iOS (primary) Price: Free

This app's top chart ranking across 50+ countries is remarkable and speaks to the power of simplicity — it gives users exactly what they need without overwhelming them. The diaspora audience (UK, USA, Canada, Australia) responds strongly to it. However, the app has not evolved significantly and sits between the power-user apps and a truly modern consumer experience. It is a digital reference book rather than an engaging daily companion. No visual moon language, no notifications, no widget for lock screen or home screen.

Strengths

  • #1 in 50+ countries — enormous distribution advantage
  • All 5 Panchanga elements; auspicious timings and major festivals
  • Offline-first; clean city/location selection
  • Approachable for users new to Panchanga
  • Strong word-of-mouth from diaspora communities

Weaknesses / Gaps

  • No Kundali, no advanced muhurta, no regional calendars
  • No moon-phase visualisation
  • No home-screen widgets or notifications
  • Limited language support (primarily English + Hindi)
  • Accuracy harder to verify — calculation method not disclosed
  • Weaker Android presence than iOS
4. Tithi — Hindu Calendar ~4.3 ★ · Niche · Android only
Platform: Android only Price: Free, no ads

This app is the closest conceptual cousin to Tithika in the market — ad-free, offline, and puts the tithi at the centre of the experience. However, it is limited to Android, has a small user base, and has not invested in design or visual storytelling. Its existence validates the market appetite for a focused, ad-free tithi app, but it has not executed on the visual and UX quality that would make it mainstream. Tithika can learn from its positioning while dramatically outclassing it on design, platform coverage, and content depth.

Strengths

  • Ad-free by design — rare in this category
  • Fully offline
  • Tithi-first design philosophy — closest to Tithika's concept
  • Supports Hindi, Gujarati, and Marathi
  • Lightweight and fast

Weaknesses / Gaps

  • Android only — excludes a large part of the target market
  • No programmatic moon visualisation
  • No notifications, muhurta, or festival detail pages
  • Limited languages (3 Indian languages, no English)
  • No home-screen widget; small community
  • Unknown developer — trust and maintenance uncertain
5. Hindu Calendar (Android) ~4.2 ★ · Moderate downloads · Android only
Platform: Android only Price: Free

A solid, workmanlike app that serves the Android market well for users who want a balanced Panchanga reference without the complexity of Drik Panchang. It is not particularly distinguished in any one dimension but avoids major failure modes. Its horoscope section is a useful retention hook. It would not be a primary competitor to Tithika given its Android-only nature and lack of differentiating design investment, but it occupies the same casual-user space that Tithika aims to serve on both platforms.

Strengths

  • Good balance of Panchanga detail and user-friendliness
  • Offline functionality — critical for areas with poor connectivity
  • Includes personalized horoscopes
  • Festival calendar with key dates
  • Generally reliable for daily use

Weaknesses / Gaps

  • Android only
  • Accuracy should be cross-verified — not authoritative
  • No moon visualisation
  • No notifications or reminders
  • UI is functional but not modern or engaging
  • No home-screen widget; limited language support

Strategic Implications for Tithika

Where Tithika Wins by Design

The competitive landscape reveals a clear white space that Tithika is positioned to own. No existing app combines: (1) tithi-first focus, (2) beautiful moon visualisation, (3) ad-free free tier, (4) open source credibility, and (5) modern consumer-grade design.

  • No moon visualisation anywhere. Not one competitor renders the moon phase visually. This is Tithika's most immediate visual differentiator — it makes the tithi tangible.
  • Ads are a universal pain point. The most-reviewed complaint across Drik Panchang is intrusive advertising. An ad-free v1 is a genuine competitive advantage.
  • Complexity is a weakness, not a feature. Users routinely complain that full-featured apps are overwhelming. Tithika's scoped feature set is a deliberate design bet — and the market evidence supports it.
  • Open source creates trust. For a devotional app where accuracy is paramount, open source means the community can verify the calculations. No competitor offers this.
  • The diaspora is underserved. Hindu Calendar–Panchang is #1 in 50+ countries without doing anything special for that segment. A beautiful, English-first, location-aware app with moon rendering would resonate powerfully.

Where Tithika Needs to Earn Trust

  • Accuracy: Drik Panchang is the accuracy standard. Tithika must match it and make that verifiable — the open-source Swiss Ephemeris foundation is a strong start, but it must be communicated clearly.
  • Festival depth: Competitors have years of festival content. Tithika's v1 list (36 festivals) is solid, but edge cases should be reviewed by practicing users before launch.
  • Language: Starting with English/Hindi is appropriate, but Devanagari rendering must be flawless on day one — any script errors will undermine trust with devout users.
  • Brand credibility: New apps lack the trust of Drik Panchang (backed by a 10+ year website). Tithika should lean into its open-source nature and accuracy claims from launch.

Tithika should position itself as "the beautiful, honest lunar calendar" — a contrast to cluttered, ad-heavy incumbents.

Key Takeaways

Drik Panchang is the authority to benchmark accuracy against — it is both the bar to meet and the warning of what to avoid (ads, complexity, visual blandness).
No competitor visualises the moon. This is Tithika's most defensible and immediate differentiator at launch.
Ad-free is rare and valued. Only niche, low-scale apps avoid ads today. Tithika's commitment to no ads at the free tier is a genuine market gap.
The diaspora market is proven and underserved by beautiful apps. Hindu Calendar–Panchang is #1 in 50+ countries doing very little — Tithika can do far more.
Open source + Swiss Ephemeris is the fastest path to accuracy credibility with the power-user segment that bloggers, priests, and community leaders belong to.
Scope is a moat. Resist the temptation to add Kundali, horoscopes, or muhurta to v1. The clear, focused product is the differentiator — bloat is the incumbent's weakness.
